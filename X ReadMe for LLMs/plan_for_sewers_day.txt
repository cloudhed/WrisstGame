# Plan: sewers_day.json — Full Pichidi Scene

## Context

`sewers_day.json` needs a complete rewrite as a multi-visit Pichidi encounter scene. Pichidi is an Ichmi sewer-dweller in active breeder→brood transition: dominant by habit, increasingly pulled toward being mounted, aware of the biology, mildly annoyed rather than distressed. The scene spans up to five visit states, a question hub, three sex routes with branching, nat20 brood moments, and a post-sex cuddle/sleep sequence that is itself a major character beat.

**entry_v5_brood_peak:** They're lower than usual. Near the drainage shelf. Different quality to the silence. They don't speak first. → brood_peak_check → main_hub

## Writing Rules to Enforce

- **Pronouns:** Pichidi uses **they/them** throughout — in narration, in choice text, everywhere. The Ichmi species cycles through roles; no fixed gender.
- **Pichidi's voice pattern:** Finnish syntax with English and Old Mire words. Articles drop. Subject-object inverts. Emotion and need are expressed as weight or pressure, not state.
  - Wrong: *"Heee... so you're back down here with me again. Couldn't let go of the experience from last time? I wouldn't mind taking your deh for a ride again."*
  - Right: *"Heee... you are back down here with me. Memory of last time is heavy on you. Is need on you to see me in sewers again? I ride your deh again, heee..."*
- **Narration nodes:** second person present tense; camera voice only. Full sentences, 8–20 words. No clipped syntax, no dropped articles, no "heee" — those belong exclusively in NPC nodes.
- **NPC nodes:** `"name": "Pichidi"` always. Old Mire terms only here. "heee" only here.
- **Cadence:** 1–2 narration nodes between choices (conversation); 1–3 (sex). Micro-choice if 3+ nodes pass without a choice.
- **Escape hatches:** present at every escalation point until penetration is established.
- **Sensory:** touch + at least one other sense in every sex narration node.
- **NPC unprompted actions:** at least one per sex route (they set a rule, change rhythm, push back).
- **Write all content fully.** No WrisstExpert placeholders — write every explicit beat directly.
