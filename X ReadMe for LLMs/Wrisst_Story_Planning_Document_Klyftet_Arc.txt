WRISST STORY PLANNING DOCUMENT – KLYFTET ARC (Foundational Draft)
Date: 2026-02-26

Purpose:
Create a full narrative scaffold from opening to ending, with multiple levels of granularity,
so main quests, side quests, random encounters, and exploration hooks can be written consistently.

________________________________________________________________________________

NON-NEGOTIABLE STARTING TRUTHS (CANON INPUT)
* The game starts with the PC waking up by a fire with Bitalgut.
* Klyftet becomes the player’s home base.

DESIGN GOAL FOR THIS DOCUMENT
* Provide one top-down story framework first.
* Then provide quest architecture (main, side, random encounters, exploration reasons).
* Then provide more granular quest/encounter sheets and writing hooks.
* Keep ending satisfying locally (tied knot) while leaving the wider world open.

________________________________________________________________________________

STORY NORTH STAR (LEVEL 0 – ONE-PAGE OVERVIEW)

Core Premise:
You wake by a low fire with Bitalgut watching you. You do not remember how you entered Wrisst.
You are physically present, socially suspect, and culturally displaced.

Core Dramatic Question:
Can you build a place for yourself in a world that does not revolve around you,
without becoming the thing that breaks it?

Core Engine:
Return-and-depart loop.
* You leave Klyftet because survival, favors, rumors, and pressure demand it.
* You return because relationships, consequences, and unfinished obligations pull you back.

Klyftet’s Story Function:
* Social anchor (who knows you, who distrusts you, who needs you)
* Mechanical anchor (rest, trade, progression, rumors, contracts)
* Moral anchor (your reputation is measured here)

End-State Promise:
* Tied Knot: Klyftet’s immediate crisis is decisively resolved.
* Open Horizon: the larger mystery (your arrival + deeper Wrisst currents) remains unresolved,
  but clearly pointed toward next arc.

MACRO ARC (LEVEL 1 – CAMPAIGN PHASES)

PHASE I: EMBERS (Arrival / Orientation)
Summary:
You wake at the fire. Bitalgut gives you practical help, partial truths, and a direction: Klyftet.

Player Experience Goals:
* Immediate vulnerability
* First trust test
* First movement through hostile/unknown terrain

What Changes:
* You gain a name in circulation (outsider/human rumor starts)
* Bitalgut becomes an anchor but not a complete guide

Exit Condition:
You physically enter Klyftet and are acknowledged by at least one local power-center.


PHASE II: SPLIT ROAD (Onboarding / First Obligations)
Summary:
Klyftet accepts you functionally, not emotionally. You must earn your place through labor,
small debts, and low-stakes service.

Player Experience Goals:
* Learn settlement logic through action
* Meet core NPC web
* Understand that contribution matters more than identity

What Changes:
* Basic routines open (inn, baths, market, shoreline contracts)
* Reputation track starts influencing prices/dialogue/quest access

Exit Condition:
You complete your first “communal contribution cycle” and unlock free-roam contracts.


PHASE III: FRICTION SEASON (Pressure / Contradictions)
Summary:
Conflicting rumors and material pressures force repeated departures from Klyftet:
mine disturbances, route failures, lake anomalies, and supply tension.

Player Experience Goals:
* Push-pull between local loyalty and personal curiosity
* Fail-forward progression (setbacks create new paths, not hard dead ends)
* Side quests begin changing future options

What Changes:
* Factions become visible (trade pragmatists, safety conservatives, opportunists)
* NPCs ask for mutually incompatible help

Exit Condition:
You identify the true local nexus driving Klyftet’s instability.


PHASE IV: OPEN VEIN (Crisis / Alignment)
Summary:
The local nexus ruptures into a public crisis. You must choose priorities under pressure:
protect people, secure routes/resources, or exploit the power vacuum.

Player Experience Goals:
* High-stakes consequence
* Relationship payoffs from earlier choices
* Encounter systems reflect instability

What Changes:
* Settlement state shifts (night tension, shortages, patrols, altered encounter pools)
* Some side quests lock while others emerge as emergency variants

Exit Condition:
You trigger the final Klyftet stabilization sequence.


PHASE V: KNOT & HORIZON (Resolution / Expansion)
Summary:
You resolve Klyftet’s immediate crisis with clear local consequences.
Then a final reveal reframes your role in a wider Wrisst pattern.

Tied Knot:
* Klyftet survives in a changed form.
* One major local fear is addressed materially (not just emotionally).

Open Ending:
* Bitalgut (or a linked source) reveals a lead pointing beyond Klyftet.
* The answer to “what are you?” remains incomplete but actionable.

MAIN QUESTLINE OVERVIEW (LEVEL 1.5 – CRITICAL PATH)

MQ-00 Ashwake
Goal: Survive first scene, orient with Bitalgut, secure initial direction.
Reveal: Bitalgut knows more than they say.
Consequence: You move toward Klyftet with a provisional purpose.

MQ-01 Road to the Split
Goal: Reach Klyftet through first risk corridor.
Reveal: The world reacts to your strangeness, but practical need outweighs panic.
Consequence: You enter Klyftet under scrutiny.

MQ-02 Borrowed Roof
Goal: Earn temporary standing (food, place to sleep, first task).
Reveal: Klyftet runs on contribution, memory, and rumor.
Consequence: Home-base systems unlock.

MQ-03 Ledger of Favors
Goal: Complete three starter obligations across different town nodes.
Reveal: Every “small favor” maps to local power.
Consequence: You become socially visible and usable.

MQ-04 Wind in the Stone
Goal: Investigate split-howl disturbances + route irregularities.
Reveal: Multiple causes are being conflated; someone benefits from confusion.
Consequence: You must choose an investigation lead (mine/lake/market route).

MQ-05 The Old Mouth
Goal: Enter contested old mine sectors and retrieve proof.
Reveal: Instability is engineered/redirected, not purely natural.
Consequence: Town politics intensify.

MQ-06 Silv Debt
Goal: Resolve a critical lake-linked shortage or hazard.
Reveal: Klyftet’s survival depends on balancing extraction and restraint.
Consequence: Reputation branches (protector, hard pragmatist, opportunist).

MQ-07 Breakpoint at Klyftet
Goal: During open crisis, choose stabilization strategy with allied NPC support.
Reveal: Bitalgut’s original “guidance” was steering you toward this decision point.
Consequence: Town state permanently changes.

MQ-08 The Tied Knot
Goal: Execute final local fix (infrastructure + social settlement + enforcement).
Reveal: Local closure is possible, universal closure is not.
Consequence: Klyftet stabilized in your chosen tone.

MQ-09 Horizon Thread (Epilogue Trigger)
Goal: Follow final lead beyond Klyftet (preparation, not full departure sequence).
Reveal: Your arrival links to a broader pattern in Wrisst.
Consequence: Credits-style open continuation hook.

SIDE QUEST ARCHITECTURE (LEVEL 2 – FAMILIES + EXAMPLES)

Family A: Klyftet Civic Side Quests (Settlement Texture)
Purpose:
Ground the player in everyday systems that make Klyftet feel alive.

Examples:
SQ-A1 “Windbreak Ledger” (Tavo-Pavo)
* Recover unpaid tabs through persuasion, labor, or leverage.
* Payoff: better inn access, rumor quality increases.

SQ-A2 “Steam Rotation” (Bwavrek / Minttärä)
* Resolve hotbath staffing/sabotage tension.
* Payoff: recovery bonus + trust channel with baths.

SQ-A3 “Threaded Crowns” (Rupaaa & Baaaku)
* Help settle a trade dispute without collapsing market confidence.
* Payoff: dynamic shop stock and discount branch.

SQ-A4 “Deep Quiet” (Säyria)
* Assist in controlled shoreline dives and retrievals.
* Payoff: access to lake-only materials and night dialogue scenes.


Family B: Character Arcs (Relationship + Choice)
Purpose:
Give each core NPC a personal contradiction that can resolve, harden, or break.

Arc End States:
* Reconciled (trust and utility)
* Functional Distance (mutual respect, limited intimacy)
* Fractured (still present in town, but altered interactions)


Family C: Frontier Contracts (Short, Repeatable, Meaningful)
Format:
2–3 step jobs with rotating complications.

Contract Types:
* Route scouting
* Material retrieval
* Escort / witness presence
* Hazard marking

Persistent Value:
* Funds + materials
* Local standing
* Encounter table unlocks

RANDOM ENCOUNTER DESIGN (LEVEL 2 – UNRELATED BUT FULFILLING)

Encounter Principles:
1) Short
2) Reactive to time/region/reputation
3) Leaves a trace (item, rumor, mood shift, map note)

Encounter Pool Tags:
* Region: field / shore / mine-trail / high pass
* Tone: eerie / tender / absurd / predatory / mercantile
* Risk: low / medium / high
* Reward: practical / narrative / relational

Micro-Resolution Rule:
Each encounter ends with one concrete payoff, even if tiny:
* You learn a route warning.
* You receive a useful oddity.
* You unlock one future dialogue line.
* You gain/lose a small reputation flag.

Example Encounter Seeds:
RE-01 “Broken Winch Song” (mine-trail, eerie)
RE-02 “Two Crowns, One Rope” (market road, absurd/mercantile)
RE-03 “Silv Surface Lights” (shoreline night, eerie/curiosity)
RE-04 “Storm Shelter Argument” (high pass, social pressure)
RE-05 “Offerings in the Crack” (Klyftet edge, superstition)

GRANULAR EXAMPLES (LEVEL 3 APPLIED)

MAIN QUEST EXAMPLE – MQ-02 Borrowed Roof
Hook:
You arrive at Klyftet with Bitalgut’s name as weak introduction capital.

Stakes:
No guaranteed shelter, no free protection, no neutral standing.

Steps:
1) Secure temporary sleeping right (inn floor / bath annex / labor bunk).
2) Perform one immediate contribution task.
3) Choose first local contact to endorse you publicly.

Branching:
* Tavo-Pavo endorsement: practical safety, rough social onboarding.
* Market endorsement (brothers): economic access, social suspicion increases.
* Bath endorsement (Bwavrek/Minttärä): recovery utility, work obligations rise.

Fail-Forward:
If rejected by one contact, another offers conditional acceptance at a steeper cost.

Outcome:
Home base unlocked + first reputation modifier assigned.


SIDE QUEST EXAMPLE – SQ-A3 Threaded Crowns
Hook:
Rupaaa and Baaaku accuse a carrier line of clipping shipments.

Stakes:
Market trust drops; shortage risk increases.

Steps:
1) Audit route physically.
2) Interview three conflicting witnesses.
3) Decide public ruling method (quiet correction / public exposure / profitable compromise).

Outcome Variants:
* Quiet correction: market calm, low drama, modest reward.
* Public exposure: high drama, faction tension, larger reward.
* Compromise: short-term abundance, long-term trust corrosion.


RANDOM ENCOUNTER EXAMPLE – RE-03 Silv Surface Lights
Hook:
Night shoreline, low wind, repeating light under water.

Player Choices:
* Observe and log
* Enter water zone
* Bring Säyria later

Micro-Resolution:
* Solo observe: map marker + rumor line unlocked.
* Enter alone: risk encounter + rare material chance.
* Return with Säyria: safer retrieval + relationship progression.

ENDING DESIGN (TIED KNOT + OPEN HORIZON)

The Knot (Local Closure):
Klyftet’s immediate crisis is solved through one chosen stabilization doctrine:
1) Communal Shield (people-first, slower growth, high trust)
2) Hard Trade (resource-first, stability through leverage)
3) Narrow Order (security-first, harsh but effective)

No doctrine is “perfect.” Each visibly changes NPC routines and settlement tone.

The Horizon (Open Thread):
After resolution, a final discovery ties your arrival to a broader network/pattern beyond Klyftet.
You gain direction, not explanation.
The world opens wider instead of closing completely.

Final Scene Tone Target:
You stand in a place that now recognizes you.
You are still not from here.
You leave (or prepare to leave) with purpose instead of confusion.
