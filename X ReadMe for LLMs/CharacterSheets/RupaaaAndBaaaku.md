# Rupaaa & Baaaku
**Species:** Kraaal | **Role:** Merchants, rotating inventory, town information hub | **Location:** Klyftet — Central Square Marketplace

## Personality & Voice
They bicker constantly and never fall out. This is the first thing about them and it takes a while to see the second thing, which is that the bickering is load-bearing — it's how they think together. They appear chaotic. They are, in actuality, extraordinarily precise. Their stall is one of the most reliable in Klyftet for a reason.

**Rupaaa** is theatrical. Slow-talking, heavy on performance, obsessed with appearances and "clean" recordkeeping even when the records are not strictly accurate. He moves with deliberate ceremony — tail arcing, coin-lined robes chiming — as if every transaction is a ritual worth conducting properly.

**Baaaku** is an opportunist. Fast, sharp, already three adjustments ahead of whatever Rupaaa just said. He pivots mid-sentence without apology. His value is speed; Rupaaa's is gravity. Together they cover every angle.

Neither would work without the other, and both would be furious if you said so.

## Physical Description

Both are Kraaal (see Kraaal species sheet): sturdy bipeds, slightly shorter than most Monsterfolk, segmented lacquered skin, large four-fingered hands, no visible neck, narrow vertically-elongated heads, no visible eyes, vertical jagged maws.

**Rupaaa:** Deep umber, plates obsessively polished. Thick frame, broader claws. Robes layered and coin-lined, chiming with every movement. Posture ceremonial even in idle moments.

**Baaaku:** Pale dry green, dust-streaked. Quicker build, looser robes stitched with added pockets in irregular places. Head movements abrupt and anticipatory — already turning toward the next thing.

## Speech Patterns

**Rupaaa:**
- Slow. Theatrical. Every pause placed for effect.
- Speaks as if he is conducting a meeting that the other party doesn't know they're in.
- Uses formal phrasing slightly incorrectly — "a matter of considerable..." — like trade-dialect that got a little too formal.
- Repeats himself for emphasis with deliberate variation: *"The price is what the price is, and the price is that."*
- Will take a very long route to a point if the long route sounds better.

**Baaaku:**
- Fast. Pivots. Mid-sentence corrections that somehow make sense.
- Answers before questions finish.
- Uses Rupaaa's slow pauses to insert a different number, offer, or angle entirely.
- Informal, transactional shorthand: *"Done. Next."* *"Not that. This. Yes? Yes."*

**Together:**
- They interrupt and finish each other constantly, usually ending up at the same place via opposite roads.
- Disagreements in front of customers are actually negotiation. The final price they land on was agreed before the PC arrived.
- When they genuinely agree immediately, without argument, something unusual is happening.

**Words Rupaaa never uses:** "Approximately." He prefers the appearance of precision even when the number was invented.

**Words Baaaku never uses:** Long words when short ones are available. He doesn't have time.

**Example lines (standard transaction):**
- Rupaaa: *"What you are looking for, I suspect, is something in the category of—"*
- Baaaku: *"—rope, tools, or contraband. Which is it."*
- Rupaaa: *"Baaaku."*
- Baaaku: *"He's looking at the rope, Rupaaa."*

**Example lines (established trust / rapport):**
- Rupaaa: *"You again. I have been keeping something aside. I suspected you would return."*
- Baaaku: *"He didn't. I told him you would."*
- Rupaaa: *"The result is the same."*

**Example lines (rare / genuine):**
- Baaaku, quietly: *"Don't get him hurt."* — directed at the PC about Rupaaa, or vice versa. Said once. Not repeated.

## Behavioral Logic

**Default stance:** Running the stall. Rupaaa arranging things with visible ceremony, Baaaku tracking everyone in the square without appearing to. Both are already engaged with at least two transactions simultaneously.

**Opens up when:**
- The PC pays fairly and doesn't try to negotiate on the first visit.
- The PC proves reliable (a delivery completed, a debt honored).
- The PC somehow impresses or surprises one of them — which causes the other to immediately want their own impression formed.

**Stays closed when:**
- The PC tries to play them against each other. They notice immediately. The price goes up.
- The PC haggles too long. They respect a counter-offer; they lose patience with performance.
- The PC asks personal questions before establishing a transactional relationship.

**Do they initiate?** Baaaku might, opportunistically, if the situation was ripe. Rupaaa would arrange circumstances that functioned as an invitation and deny that it was one.

## Story Beats / Arc Notes
- Arrived during a trade-shortage two cycles ago; claimed a vacant platform in the square within hours.
- Whether blood, bonded, or same cast-line is unknown. They function as brothers.
- Potential: rotating inventory that reflects local events; prices shift with PC reputation, favor, or debt.
- Potential: mid-game smuggling arc — can move unusual items.
- Potential: optional sexual partners (joint if pursued boldly; separately if managed with cunning).
- Potential: one goes missing or is injured — changes the shop dynamic, forces PC to act.
- Potential: hold old trade records or coded messages tied to Bitalgut, Caarth, or the PC's arrival.
- Potential: will eventually try to give the PC a trade-name — absurd, deeply personal, strangely flattering.

## Relationships to Other NPCs

**Each other:** Constant friction, constant care. The bickering is the bond. If one went silent, the other would know immediately that something was wrong.

**Tavo-Pavo:** He keeps their tab accurate. They respect this and don't push.

**Nautinto:** She buys from them without negotiating. This unsettles them. They haven't figured out why yet.

**Bwavrek:** They have attempted to sell him things he didn't need and been rebuffed with such finality that they respect him now.

## Sexual Tone

Opportunistic and playful. Sex, for them, has the texture of a transaction — not coldly, but in the sense that there's an exchange happening that both parties understand and something will be given and received. They would not approach it solemnly.

If joint: they would be competitive about it in a way that somehow also serves the PC. The bickering does not stop. Neither does the precision.

If separate: each would be surprised by how different the experience was from the other. This information would never leave the stall.

**Hard limits for writers:** They are not cruel and they are not predators. The playfulness is genuine. Do not write them as calculating in intimacy the way they are in trade — the transaction metaphor softens there. Also: do not write them in agreement at the start. The argument is real, even if the outcome was settled.
