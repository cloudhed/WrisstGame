# Kraaal
**Type:** Monsterfolk | **Common locations:** Market clusters, crossroad villages, travel stops — wherever trade flows

## Overview
The go-everywhere merchants of Wrisst. Always running something: stall, ferry point, toll hut, storage barn. Reputation-driven, precision-organized, and culturally obsessed with fair exchange — though a well-executed scam, if it happens, is done with elegance and rarely repeated on the same mark.

## Physical Description
Sturdy bipeds, slightly shorter than most Monsterfolk. Skin segmented, lacquered in glossy earth tones: umber to pale citrine. No visible neck; hunched posture makes large four-fingered hands look even larger. Three claws on each strong reptilian foot. Heads narrow, smooth, vertically elongated — no visible eyes. Mouths are the most striking feature: vertical jagged maws that take up the entire face. Doesn't inhibit communication, but makes emotional reading nearly impossible without knowing their body-language cues. They snarl as they speak — this is not hostility.

**Caste variation:** No distinct sexes observed. "Deep-caste" Kraaal are broader, larger-clawed, slower speech. "Trade-line" Kraaal are thinner, smaller, quicker in movement.

**Clothing:** Many wear layers of coin-lined cloth that jingles when they move.

**Genitalia (Note to self #2):** Plates part low on the belly revealing a soft teal-colored slit — smooth, almost glowing. Opens into a supple fold structure, wet-looking, gently pulsing. No obvious sex marker — a receiving sheath that shifts shape slightly. Closes slowly afterward, plates sealing like it was never there. Clean scent.

## Behavior
- Often the first to approach, rarely the first to trust.
- Speak multiple trade dialects; simulate warmth through practiced tone.
- Obsessively organized — track transactions by scratch, code, and scent-stamp.
- Reputation-driven: a Kraaal without a network is "unbacked" and shunned.
- Deep cultural reverence for fair exchange. Scams, when executed, are precise, elegant, and not repeated on the same mark.
- Frequently nomadic; "stationed" Kraaal often act as civic anchors in smaller towns.

## Writing Notes
- Voice is practiced warmth over transactional precision. They sound friendly until you realize every word has been budgeted.
- Body language matters more than their face — you cannot read a Kraaal's expression; you read their posture and tail.
- PC observation (in-character): "Why does every village have at least two of these guys? Always running something... They move like they already know what you're going to ask for, and they've priced it ten ways before you speak."
