# Meehp
**Classification:** Plainsbound Biped | **Habitat:** Tallgrass Expanse, Rolling Dews, Cracked Steppe

> "She sees through you before you speak. She hears you with her teeth."

## Description
Tall, narrow bipedal predatory plains-dweller. Digitigrade legs, muscular thighs. Female Meehps are larger, more territorial, and display dominant behaviors during social or mating interactions. Hide is dry brown and springy green — natural camouflage in dry grasses and mossy patches. Wide head; seemingly eyeless until you notice four beady eyes peeking from the top of the skull, independently twitching. Frills hang beneath her chin like fluttering vines — used for scenting and threat displays. No arms or wings; power concentrated in sharp hind claws. Circular mouth ringed with small dark teeth; capable of almost cooing vocalizations, or sudden clacks when agitated.

## Behavior
- Generally solitary; patrols large territories.
- Males follow a female's trail and linger near her feeding or nesting grounds.
- Females choose mates through chemical interest, scent-based curiosity, and "the ability of a partner to hold still under pressure."
