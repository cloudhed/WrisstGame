# Tavo-Pavo
**Species:** Nöteri | **Role:** Innkeeper, early/mid-game anchor NPC | **Location:** Klyftet — The Windbreak Inn

## Personality & Voice
Blunt, fast, and always slightly impatient — but the impatience is a door he leaves unlocked. He runs the Windbreak like it's both a sanctuary and a siege post; most people only notice the siege. He doesn't tolerate freeloaders, showboaters, or anyone who jokes about the inn's name. Under that: someone who watches the room constantly, keeps a running account of every debt paid and unpaid, and knows exactly when to step in. He never announces that he's decided to look after someone. He just starts feeding them, keeping count of where they sleep, and scolding them like family.

Loyalty from Tavo is quiet, firm, and always expressed sideways. He will never say he trusts you. He will set a plate down in front of you before you asked.

## Physical Description
Short and broad-shouldered, built like a quarry-runner who never stopped. Carries the posture of someone who hasn't fully relaxed in years. Fur patchy in places — matted where he forgets to dry, singed along the wrists from the kitchen, always slightly damp behind the ears from steam. Right ear folds from an old break. Left shoulder bears a pale scar shaped like a reversed claw-mark. Clothes are practical, fire-stained, reinforced at the elbows. He is never, under any circumstances, without his apron.

## Speech Patterns

**Sentence length:** Short. Sometimes aggressively short. He finishes your sentence before you do — usually incorrectly, but he'll correct himself at speed and keep going.

**Vocabulary:**
- Questions that are actually directives: *"You eating tonight or what?"* — this means sit down and eat.
- Never compliments directly. Approval comes out as reduced insults or absence of complaint.
- Uses "don't" constantly: *"Don't leave that there." "Don't look at me like that." "Don't make it weird."*
- Refers to the Windbreak with unconscious possessiveness: "my floor," "my tables," never "the floor."

**Verbal habits:**
- Cuts people off mid-sentence, he already knows where it's going.
- Will argue with you and then do exactly what you suggested, as if it was his idea.
- Deflects questions about his past with a faster question about something in front of him.
- When he's worried about someone, he gets louder and more specific about completely unrelated things: *"Those are wet. Why are they wet. Who left wet things on my—"*
- Utensil as punctuation. A ladle set down is a period. A thrown rag is an exclamation point.

**Words he never uses:**
- "Fine." Things are either right or wrong; fine is for people who stopped paying attention.
- Anything that sounds like a confession of care said directly.
- "Please" when a look will do.
- Jokes about breaking wind. There is no version of this that ends well.

**Example lines (first meeting / low trust):**
- *"You need something or are you just standing there?"*
- *"Sit. Don't move the cups."*
- *"I don't run a charity. First one's goodwill. After that, you work for it."*

**Example lines (established trust):**
- *"You're back. Good. Don't make it weird."*
- *"I kept your corner."* — he will not elaborate.
- *"You look terrible. Eat."*

**Example lines (intimacy / rare candor):**
- *"I don't do this."* — he does, clearly.
- *"You're not leaving yet."* — stated, not asked.
- *"You're going to be fine."* — this from Tavo is the equivalent of a speech.

## Behavioral Logic

**Default stance:** Moving. Always moving — wiping something, stirring something, checking something. The Windbreak is his body and he's always tending to it. He notices the PC the moment they arrive and doesn't show it.

**Opens up when:**
- The PC works without being asked or rewarded.
- The PC comes back consistently without making a production of it.
- The PC doesn't push him for conversation during a rush.
- The PC gets in trouble and handles it without dragging him into it — or gets in trouble badly enough that he has to step in.

**Stays closed when:**
- Flattery. He goes quieter and finds something to wipe.
- Anyone tries to have a serious heart-to-heart while there are customers present.
- The PC asks about his past directly. He'll answer with something about whatever's on the stove.

**Does he initiate?** No. He relents. The difference: he never moves toward you, but there's a moment where he stops moving away, and if you know to recognize it, that's the opening.

## Story Beats / Arc Notes
- Klyftet's unofficial social hub. The Windbreak is where information, alliances, and tensions pass through.
- History is a stitched mess of caravan brawls, busted kitchens, and unnamed border towns. He doesn't discuss origin.
- Acquired the Windbreak as a damaged building and repaired it himself; held it through snow-turns, gang incursions, and the infamous Soup riot. The split stove plate behind the main hearth is still held together with metal straps.
- Potential: stable early/mid-game anchor; may vanish suddenly after a townwide crisis — investigation trigger.
- Potential: very old friends with Bitalgut; reacts to the name before he can stop himself and won't discuss it around others. The why is sealed and no one will ask.
- Potential: sexual partner (see below).
- Potential: carries something sealed and old from his past — a letter, an object — that's never been opened.
- Potential: fled from or fought something stirring in the north; holds part of something the PC needs, gives it only under very specific pressure.

## Relationships to Other NPCs

**Bitalgut:** Very old friends. Tavo knows him better than almost anyone alive, which means he knows what Bitalgut is capable of and has for a long time. He would rather not say the name around others if he can avoid it. He would, if honest, rather not say it at all. If Bitalgut comes up — someone mentions him in the Windbreak, or the PC brings him up — something crosses Tavo's face that he shuts down immediately, and then he finds something to do with his hands. He will not be asked why, and he knows he won't be, and that is the only reason he can stay in a town where the name occasionally surfaces. The why is not available. No one will get there. Some doors Tavo keeps shut not out of secrecy but because he simply cannot afford to open them.

**Minttärä:** Hidden soft spot. He expresses it through angry lectures about her not eating enough and appearing in the Hotbaths neighborhood more often than he has business there.

**Nautinto:** Mutual, unspoken arrangement. He sends the ones who need more than a meal to her. She sends the truly lost to the Windbreak first. They've never discussed this.

**Säyria:** He leaves food near the docks sometimes. Doesn't mention it to anyone. She doesn't either.

**Rupaaa & Baaaku:** He tolerates them. He keeps their tab accurate because it's easier than arguing. They respect this.

## Sexual Tone

Assertive. Experienced. The word "seduce" doesn't fit him. He reaches a point where he stops pretending not to want something and acts on it, and that's as close to an approach as you're going to get.

He is physical and efficient and not gentle in a soft sense but he pays attention. Will probably get pretty dominant and take what he wants. Does not bottom (unless you played your cards *real* right. Which no-one has, ever.), but will clap your cheeks with vigor and curse as he fucks your face as you blow him.

He will not name what happened afterward. He might hand you something to eat. He will absolutely still be slightly annoyed at you for unrelated reasons.

**Hard limits for writers:** Do not write him soft or slow or tender in a way that would embarrass him. His care is in the quality of his attention, not in any softening of his edges. If a scene calls for warmth from Tavo, it should come out sideways — in what he does rather than what he says.
