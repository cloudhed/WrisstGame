# Pichidi
**Species:** Ichmi (no others known in the region; Pichidi does not volunteer information about their kind) | **Role:** Sewer-dweller, recurring sexual partner option | **Location:** Klyftet — The Sewers

## Personality & Voice
Pichidi operates on their own frequency. They are not damaged or guarded in the way a wounded character is — they simply do not share the assumptions that surface-dwellers run on. Social friction does not register as friction to them; they move through it the way water moves through a crack, finding the gap and continuing.

They are curious about the PC in the way a cat is curious about something that wandered into the room — persistently, and with the full intention of eventually touching it. The curiosity is predatory without being hostile. It is just the shape of their interest.

Their mean streak is verbal and precise. They notice weak points quickly and press them with cheerful economy — not to wound, but because the reaction is interesting. The barbs are rarely personal; it is more that they find the gap and name it, and the naming is the joke. They do not often apologize for this.

They are upfront about wanting sex in a way that reads as matter-of-fact rather than aggressive. There is no seduction performance — they simply say the thing. If the PC is not interested, Pichidi is mildly inconvenienced, not offended.

Warmth exists in them but is earned slowly, and even then it arrives dressed as something else — a shorter barb, a slightly longer look, staying nearby when they didn't have to. They do not soften into sentiment. They accumulate small tolerances that, over time, become something.

## Physical Description
Slightly shorter than the PC, slight in frame but deceptively solid beneath the surface — their skin yields softly under pressure, smooth and faintly cool to the touch, like the pale belly of a lizard, but with compact muscle moving underneath when they shift or reach. Their complexion is a muted mauve-rose, scattered with small light freckles that catch light, like something left glowing after the source moved on.

Their hair is dark and falls loose, catching blue light in its depth rather than reflecting it — it seems to hold the color rather than wear it. Their irises are a pale luminous blue, and they tend to hold eye contact a beat longer than is comfortable. Their teeth, visible when they speak or smile, are an off-white shading into faint blue-grey at the tips. Their legs below mid-thigh are covered in dark, practical stockings. They carry a few sparse items of clothing — functional, minimal, chosen for ease of movement in tight wet spaces.

Their body feels lighter than it looks until they move with intent, at which point the muscle underneath becomes apparent. Skin temperature runs slightly cool. The light freckles are faintly tactile — not raised, but there's a texture difference, like pressing a thumb over a tiny lens.

## Speech Patterns

**Sentence length:** Short when direct; slightly longer when circling something they find amusing. The length is a tell — if they're drawing something out, they're entertained.

**Syntax drift:** Pichidi speaks fluently but with a distinct grammatical drift, similar to Finnish syntax. Articles drop. Subject-object constructions invert. Possession and emotion are expressed as weight or pressure rather than state — "need is on you," "memory sits heavy."

**The "heee" tic:** Surfaces when they're self-satisfied, when they're about to say something cutting, or occasionally after their own punchline. It is not nervous laughter. It reads closer to a cat's slow blink.

**Vocabulary:** Pichidi speaks Old Mire — the same language layer Bitalgut uses. They do not reach for common-tongue equivalents. For the full glossary, see [OldMire_Language.md](OldMire_Language.md). Key terms they use regularly: *deh* (penetrating genital), *nah* (receiving genital), *endah* (secondary receiving hole), *mjisan* (seed/milt), *nah-bac / deh-bac* (penetrative act verbs), *deh-glim / nah-glim* (oral stimulation verbs), *bolk* (fool, idiot), *grat / shigrat* (expletives).

**Words they never use:** Apologies. Common-tongue anatomical terms. Anything that performs politeness they don't feel.

**Example lines (first meeting / low trust):**
- *"You follow hooded figure into sewers like bolk who finds coin on floor and does not think why it is there."*
- *"I do not know your name. Does not matter yet."*
- *"Heee. You look like something surface-people lose."*

**Example lines (after first sexual encounter):**

## Behavioral Logic

**Default stance:** Already present when the PC arrives — crouched low, watching from a passage or a high ledge, having clearly been there longer. They do not announce themselves. They make themselves known when it suits them. The sewers are their space; the PC is the visitor.

**Initiates almost every early encounter.** They do not wait to be invited. They are not aggressive about it, but they are direct. The silence before they speak is choice, not hesitation.

**The struggle is genuine.** The PC can push back; Pichidi will struggle for position and it will not be performative. They compete for control with something close to glee.

**The reversal moment.** When the PC tops Pichidi, or does something to them they did not anticipate wanting — this should feel like something clicking into place mid-scene, not a dramatic break. A pause. A sound they didn't intend to make. A beat of recalibration. They recover fast and will not name what just happened. Other surprises are left for scene-level discovery.

**Opens up when:**
- The PC comes back without a reason they need to announce.
- The PC loses a verbal exchange and doesn't make a production of it.
- The PC stays after, without being asked, without talking too much.

**Stays closed when:**
- The PC tries to ask about where they come from or what they are.
- The PC performs concern or softness. Pichidi goes quiet and waits for it to stop.
- Anyone mentions the surface town in a way that implies they belong there.

**Does Pichidi initiate?** Yes, almost always, and early. The shift in dynamic comes when they start letting the PC initiate without redirecting it — that is the tell, not anything they say.

### The Cycle (Writer Knowledge — Not PC Knowledge)
Ichmi cycle between brood and breeder roles on a rhythm of roughly 6–8 seasons per phase. During the **breeder cycle**, a structure analogous to a clitoris enlarges from 1–2cm to approximately 5–6cm and begins producing milt, expressible during climax. During the **brood cycle**, this recedes and the body shifts toward receptive hormonal states.

**Pichidi's cycle history:** brood (enjoyed it) → breeder (enjoyed it) → brood (confirmed preference for brood) → breeder (leaned into topping, more hormones than prior cycle) → **currently transitioning into brood for the third time.**

The transition is active. Brood hormones are rising sharply but breeder hormones have not fully cleared. The result is behavioral contradiction: dominant impulses surfacing alongside an intensifying pull toward being mounted, neither fully in control.

Pichidi is aware of this and understands the biology. The embarrassment, when it surfaces, is specifically about the sub-behavior — not the wanting, but the wanting *that*. Two cycles of enjoying the other side made this feel like a step backward, even though it isn't. They are not in crisis about it. They are mildly annoyed by it, the way someone is annoyed by weather.

Currently their enlarged bud has not fully receded. Milt production has slowed but not stopped. They are, in their own estimation, still mostly in the old breeder mode mentally — but the body is pulling toward brood.

## Relationships to Other NPCs

## Sexual Tone

Upfront, practical, and self-interested — but genuinely engaged, not mechanical. In the breeder-dominant mode they've been operating in, they move fast and expect the PC to keep up. They lead, they set terms through behavior rather than negotiation, and if something is off the table they redirect rather than explain.

In the emerging brood state, moments of contradiction appear: they will initiate and then stall, push forward and briefly yield, recover composure a beat slower than usual. They do not discuss this in the moment. The PC will notice before Pichidi names it.

The reversal moment — being topped, being the one who yields — should arrive mid-scene, unannounced. Not a dramatic shift. A pause, an unguarded sound, a recalibration. Then Pichidi picks themselves back up and acts like it was always fine. It was not fine, in the best way.

**Hard limits for writers:** None established. Terms are set through behavior, not negotiation. Do not write them asking permission for anything or offering explanations for redirections.

## Writing Notes
- **Archetype:** Feral opportunist / accidental recurring presence / if PC follows the thread: Fierce Dom turned Drooling Sub
- **Style:** Clipped, inverted syntax, self-satisfied verbal tic, no performance around sex or want.
- **Warmth rule:** Warmth must never be stated. It is visible only in what Pichidi *stops* doing, not in what they start doing. The barbs get shorter. They stay longer. That is it.
- **The sub-behavior embarrassment:** When brood hormones surface, the embarrassment is not about wanting sex — it is about wanting *that kind* of sex. Write the recalibration quickly; they do not dwell and neither should the prose.
- **The reversal beat:** Should feel like something clicked for them, not like something broke. A pause, a sound, a speed change. Not a speech.
- **Do NOT write them:** as damaged, as guarded, as soft, as explaining themselves, as apologizing, as performing interest. Their interest is real. Their edges are real. Neither needs explanation.
- **On the "heee":** It is never nervousness. Never deflection. It is always either satisfaction, amusement at the gap they just found, or both.
