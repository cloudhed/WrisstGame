# Klyftet
**Type:** Frontier mountain-pass town | **Population:** ~200 permanent souls | **Setting:** Between the Gorm- and Jorm Ranges, adjacent to Lake Silv

## Overview
A frontier town carved into the split of two steep jagged mountain ranges. Buildings are rough but solid: stone foundations, wood or scrap-reinforced upper structures, sharply slanted roofs to deflect wind. Cables and simple crank-machines stretch between some buildings. The central square is broad, flattened stone with trade stalls and a speaking platform. The wind here has its own voice — locals call it the "split-howl."

## Atmosphere
Brisk, practical, communal. Strength, skill, and contribution matter more than species, size, or background. Reputation travels fast: a good name opens doors, a bad one freezes you out. Outsiders are watched carefully but not immediately distrusted — the town has seen all kinds. Communal solutions are favored over strict law; troublemakers are exiled, not imprisoned. Trade is almost entirely in Öre; Crowns are rare; Drots are never seen.

The wind: "It scrapes voices across the stones, carrying snatches of words you're sure you didn't say. Some days it's easy to laugh it off. Other nights, when the fires burn low and the wind threads through the gaps in the walls, you swear the split-howl knows your name." Keep an ear out near the narrowest part of the gorge — the wind almost sounds like it's talking.

Split-howl superstition is common: some Monsterfolk leave small offerings in cracks between stones to "appease" the wind.

## Notable Locations

**The Central Square**
The town's heart — traders, scavengers, ore-runners. Rupaaa and Baaaku (Kraaal brothers) run the most prominent supply and trade stall, constantly bickering but unbeatable when working together.

**The Windbreak Inn**
Rough, lively. Drinking, eating, heated gossip fill the evenings. Owned by Tavo-Pavo (loud-mouthed Nöteri), who is famously quick to hurl a utensil at anyone making jokes about "breaking wind." Serves as Klyftet's informal court, message hub, and sanctuary against the split-howl.

**Lake Silv Shoreline**
Wide shimmering lake to the south edge of town. Reflects the mountains. Stays unfrozen most seasons. Fishing, washing, minor trade runs. Monsterfolk treat it with respect and superstition. Selenna (Sleid) free-dives here for sjölfisk, treating the lake with near-religious reverence. Old fishing rigs and drying racks. Quieter than town.

**The Hotbaths**
Stone-walled, spring-fed bathhouse. Miners and workers clean up, socialize, and negotiate small trades. Strictly non-sexual communal space (for most). Minttärä works here under Bwavrek. Gossip overheard here is some of the most reliable intelligence in Klyftet.

**The Old Mine**
The mine Klyftet was built around — now a fenced-off lot of broken tools, discarded gear, dangerous tunnels, and abandoned smelter wagons. Some say haunted; most treat it as a scavenger's hunting ground. As many reasons for its closure as there are voices in Klyftet. The Murrisk is found in its depths.

**Nautinto's Hall**
Reclaimed ruin-turned-communal hall on the outskirts of town. Food, cloth, and stories shared freely. Quiet, welcoming to wanderers and outcasts.

## Klyftet Social Notes
- Mining equipment is scattered everywhere (hand tools, cog-cranes, oil-fed winches, broken pickaxes).
- Most mining happens up the slopes beyond town via winding rough trails.
- Flora clings stubbornly to rocks: low hardy shrubs, silvery grasses, pale-barked trees bent by constant wind.
