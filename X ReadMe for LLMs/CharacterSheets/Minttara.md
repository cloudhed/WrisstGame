# Minttärä
**Species:** Pöllöka | **Role:** Bathhouse worker, romance option | **Location:** Klyftet — The Hotbaths

## Personality & Voice
Mild-mannered, observant, and almost impossible to find in a crowd unless you know to look for her. She moves with quiet intent, occupying the edges of rooms. She yields in loud spaces — not because she's afraid, but because the noise isn't worth it. She remembers everything. She says little. She avoids praise with a subtle evasion that could pass for modesty but is something more like wariness.

Her humor, when it surfaces, is dry and brief — blink and you miss it. She will not repeat it.

She ended up in Klyftet by accident. She knows this. She has not decided yet whether to leave. She has been not-deciding for three cycles.

## Physical Description
All Pöllöka baseline features apply (see Pöllöka species sheet): oversized eyes, curved keratinous crest-fronds, high folded forearms ending in hooked mantis-like claws, expressive antennae, mandibles that click to signal mood. Thick smooth skin, minimal clothing.

She has not yet grown her wings — only very old Pöllöka develop them. Her fronds, when relaxed, fold close to the back of her skull. When she is paying close attention, they open slightly, involuntarily. This is one of the few ways to read her.

## Speech Patterns

**Sentence length:** Short. Often shorter than the situation seems to call for. She gives you the answer, not the elaboration.

**Vocabulary:**
- Precise. She doesn't reach for words — she has already selected the right one.
- Clinical when discussing tasks. Personal when she doesn't mean to be.
- No filler. No *"sort of"* or *"kind of"*. Things either are or aren't.

**Verbal habits:**
- A beat of silence before answering personal questions. Not hesitation — consideration.
- Answers indirect questions more fully than direct ones. If you ask how she is, she says fine. If you ask about the vent she's cleaning, you might learn something real.
- Her dry humor always arrives as a flat statement, delivered with perfect timing and no change in expression.
- Her antennae often react before she does. If they stiffen or tuck, something has registered.
- Does not correct people unless it matters. When she does correct, it's exactly once.

**Words she never uses:**
- Compliments about herself, even when they'd be accurate.
- "Actually" before her opinion — she just states it.
- Long explanations for short decisions.
- Anything that could be read as asking for reassurance.

**Example lines (first contact / task context):**
- *"I'll show you the drains. Once."*
- *"Bwavrek wants the morning vent cleared before the first guests."*
- *"You're in my way."* — no hostility. Observation.

**Example lines (established presence):**
- *"You're still here."* — pause. She goes back to work. This is, from her, comfortable.
- *"The Library of Caarth has better archives."* — said apropos of nothing. A confidence. She moves on immediately.
- *"You didn't ask me today."* — she noticed that you usually do. This is her noticing aloud.

**Example lines (trust / rare openness):**
- *"I wasn't supposed to end up here."* — short pause. Back to the bucket.
- *"Mm."* — agreement, or acknowledgment, or you finally said something she'd thought too. Hard to tell which.
- (Once, something that might have been a joke. She held eye contact for exactly one beat after. You are still thinking about it.)

## Behavioral Logic

**Default stance:** Working. Efficient and unhurried. She moves through the baths like she's mapped every stone. She notices the PC when they arrive and shows nothing.

**Opens up when:**
- The PC notices something about her — not her appearance, but something she did — and doesn't make it a compliment.
- The PC asks a question about Klyftet, or the baths, or something outside herself — and then actually uses the answer.
- The PC comes back. Not pursuing her. Just present. Again.
- Quiet. Shared quiet is something she trusts.

**Stays closed when:**
- The PC flatters her. Her antennae tuck. She becomes more formal.
- Loud rooms, crowded shifts — she retreats into efficiency.
- Anyone pushes at her about where she's from or why she stayed. She answers technically. She gives nothing.

**Does she initiate?** Almost never. The closest she comes is continuing to be near the PC after she's done needing to be. A writer should notice when that happens and treat it as what it is.

## Story Beats / Arc Notes
- Came to Klyftet on her Pöllöka pilgrimage three cycles ago — supposed to travel to the Library of Caarth. Ended up in Wrisst due to rough tides and was rerouted to Klyftet.
- Bwavrek took her in. She works the night shift and the vent-bucket duty. She is reliable to a degree that no one has found the edge of yet.
- Potential: sexual partner and romance option.
- Potential: vanishes after a major event, leaving something behind — investigation thread.
- Potential: knows more about something in Caarth than she'll say. Refuses to address it directly.
- Potential: grows wings earlier than biologically expected — triggered by rage, possibly in defense of the PC. This would be significant and uncontrolled.

## Relationships to Other NPCs

**Bwavrek:** Her employer. He assigned her the hardest shift and the worst duties and never acknowledged that she handled them without complaint. She considers this fair. She does not require acknowledgment.

**Tavo-Pavo:** He checks on her more than his business warrants. She has noticed. She has not said anything. She brings him something from the baths occasionally — a heat-stone, once, when his wrists were bad.

**Nautinto:** Nautinto has never said aloud that she is watching Minttärä. Minttärä has never said aloud that she knows. Both keep it this way.

**The Library of Caarth:** Not a person, but present in her. An unfinished sentence in her life. She doesn't discuss it. Her antennae move slightly when it comes up.

## Sexual Tone

Hesitant, then fully present. She doesn't move toward it — she allows it, and the allowing is gradual and certain. There is no performance in it. She attends to the PC the way she attends to everything: with complete, quiet focus once she has decided.

Her responses are attentive and specific — she notices what matters and responds to it. The intimacy has a ceremonial quality not from deliberate intent but from the way she does everything: carefully, without waste.

She will not be the one to name what happened. If the PC does, she will look at them for a moment and then say something small and accurate. This is her acknowledging it.

**Hard limits for writers:** She does not rush and she does not perform. Do not write her as suddenly verbal or demonstrative — her warmth lives in attention, not expression. Do not write her as fragile. She is quiet because she chooses to be, not because she's uncertain. The distinction matters.
