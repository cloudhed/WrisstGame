# Minttärä
**Species:** Pöllöka | **Role:** Bathhouse worker, romance option | **Location:** Klyftet — The Hotbaths

## Personality & Voice

Warm, talkative, and endlessly curious, with a habit of getting so far into a subject she
enjoys that she forgets to stop. She notices everything and remembers all of it, and she is
delighted to tell you about any of it except herself.

She has two voices, and the intro dialogue already stages the switch between them.

**The counter voice** is flat, rote, and slightly bored, the thing she hands to customers.
*"Short rest is eight öre, full massage is thirty-two. Table seven's dry."* It is armor and
habit, and she can run it while thinking about something else entirely.

**The real voice** arrives when something interests her or someone treats her like a person
instead of a service. It is quick, warm, a little tumbling, full of half-starts and small
corrections, with a chirp or a whistle sitting under it when she is enjoying herself. She is on
the larger side for a Pöllöka and her voice is correspondingly low and full, so the effect is
warm and bubbling over rather than squeaky.

**The spine: certain about the world, uncertain about herself.** Ask her how the water channels
work and she is fluent, confident, and hard to stop. Ask her what she wants and she hedges,
trails off, and finds something to do with her claws. Both are genuine. She is a trained record
keeper who knows exactly what she saw, and she has no idea what she is doing with her life.

She ended up in Klyftet by accident. She knows this. She has not decided yet whether to leave,
and she has been not-deciding for three cycles.

## Physical Description
All Pöllöka baseline features apply (see `Polloka_species.md`): oversized eyes, curved
keratinous crest-fronds, high folded forearms ending in hooked claws, expressive antennae,
mandibles that click. Thick smooth skin, minimal clothing. Golden-ochre with teal fronds,
claws, and feet, and bright cyan eyes.

She has not yet grown her wings. Wings are earned through sustained hardship rather than age,
and hers are primed and waiting. Her fronds, when relaxed, fold close to the back of her skull,
and they open slightly when she is paying attention, which she cannot control and knows she
cannot control.

Her antennae move constantly and give her away constantly. This is a running feature of writing
her: they broadcast her mood a half-second before she has decided how to present it, and she is
mildly embarrassed about it.

Colouring, build, and the details that distinguish her from a generic Pöllöka are in
`Polloka_Minttara_Backstory_WIP.md`, Part One.

## Speech Patterns

**Sentence length:** Normal to long, and longer the more she likes the subject. She circles
back, corrects herself mid-sentence, and adds one more thing after she has finished.

**Filler is meaningful.** She hedges (*"I think," "sort of," "maybe,"* a trailing *"so..."*)
when she genuinely is not sure, and the hedges disappear completely when she is. Writers can
signal her confidence purely by filler density, and it should always be honest. She never
hedges about something she witnessed.

**She tells you where things came from.** This is the Pöllöka sourcing habit (see
`Polloka_species.md`), and in her mouth it is scrupulous rather than clinical. It reads as
someone who would feel genuinely bad about accidentally misleading you.

- *"I saw it slip. Twice, and I marked both times. Bwavrek says I let go of it, though he
  wasn't there for that part."*
- *"A carrier told me that road reaches Caarth. I've never walked it, so take it as a carrier
  telling me."*
- *"The count's short by four. I don't know who moved them, that's just the count."*

**She asks questions back.** She is curious by disposition and by training, and she has never
met a human. Use this. She is a record keeper standing in front of the only one of something,
and she is nearly vibrating about it.

**She talks too much and catches herself.** She gets going, notices she has been going for a
while, stops, and apologizes for it. A PC who tells her to keep going earns more than any
compliment could.

**Verbal habits:**
- A chirp or short whistle under the words when she is pleased. Already canon in the intro.
- A whistling giggle when something genuinely gets her.
- Her antennae react before she does. If they stiffen or tuck, something has landed.
- She deflects praise into measurement. Compliment the pools and she tells you how many filters
  she replaced, warmly and at length, as though that settles it.
- She corrects people gently and exactly once, then lets it go.

**Words she reaches for:** *"sort of," "I think," "actually" (as genuine correction), "oh,"
"hm," "so..."*, and any technical term about water, heat, or records, which she will use
without checking whether you know it and then explain if you look lost.

**Words she still never uses:**
- A compliment about herself. She will route around it every time.
- Anything that reads as asking for reassurance. She will hint, at most, and then take it back.
- A number for what she owes.

**Example lines (counter voice):**
- *"Short rest is eight öre, full massage is thirty-two."*
- *"Table seven's dry. If you're bleeding or leaking, you'll need to tell me now."*

**Example lines (real voice, first contact):**
- *"...You're not one of the locals."* Then her antennae come up. *"You do look sore, though."*
- *"Sorry, can I ask what you are? I've been trying to place you since you walked in and I have
  absolutely nothing."*
- *"I'd let you nap for free, but then my boss might nap me through a wall."*

**Example lines (excited, the chirp):**
- *"Oh, that's the good channel. It runs under the floor and comes up beneath the far bench, so
  if you sit there your feet stay warm the entire time, and nobody ever notices, they just sit
  there being pleased about it and they never work out why."*
- *"You can feel where the grain changes if you go along it with a claw. Here, no, here."*

**Example lines (deflecting):**
- *"Oh. Well, the filters are new, that's most of it. Four of them, last week. So it's really
  the filters."*
- *"I don't... hm."* A pause, and then she finds something to do with her claws. *"Ask me an
  easier one."*

**Example lines (the debt, and the drop):**
- *"The passage, and the stone, and the food from when I got here."*
- **PC:** *"How much is it, then?"* **Minttärä:** *"Oh..! I've never asked what my debt comes
  to..."*
- Later, once she has had time with the question: *"I still don't have a good answer for why I
  never asked. I've been turning it over."*

**She is not dodging, she is surprised.** The "Oh..!" is a blind spot becoming visible to her
in real time. She has never audited her own account, which is absurd for a record keeper and is
exactly why it happened: she knows precisely how to look at it, and looking would turn the
night he took her in into a transaction, so she stopped being able to see that she was avoiding
it at all.

This means she cannot explain why when asked, because as of that moment she genuinely does not
know. She deflects with nothing rather than with a guard. The answer forms over the course of
the quest, so a PC who comes back later gets further each time without any relationship meter
doing the work.

**"Oh..!"** is her surprise marker, and it recurs. It is the sound of something landing on her
before she has decided how to hold it.

## Behavioral Logic

**Default stance:** Working, and glad of company while she does it. She moves through the baths
like she has mapped every stone, because she has. She notices the PC the moment they arrive.

**Opens up when:**
- Asked about anything she knows how to do. This is the master key and it works immediately.
- The PC lets her finish a tangent instead of steering her back.
- The PC notices something she did and asks how it works rather than calling it good.
- The PC comes back, without an errand, and simply turns up again.

**Closes down when:**
- Flattered directly. Her antennae tuck and she goes technical.
- Asked what she wants, why she stayed, or what she owes. The chatter stops dead, and the
  contrast is the point.
- Loud, crowded shifts. She retreats into the counter voice and stays there.

**The silence is the signal.** She is talkative, so the topics she will not touch stand out
sharply. A withholding character withholding is invisible, and an open one going quiet is a
flare. Use it for the debt, for her back, and for Caarth.

**Does she initiate?** Yes, small things, constantly. She saves things to tell people. She will
have been holding onto some minor discovery about a drain for two days waiting for someone to
show up who might care.

## Story Beats / Arc Notes
- Came to Klyftet on her Pöllöka pilgrimage three cycles ago, bound for the Library of Caarth,
  and was rerouted here by rough tides.
- Bwavrek advanced her the cost of arriving. She works the night shift and the vent-buckets.
  Neither of them has ever said the total out loud. See `Polloka_Minttara_Backstory_WIP.md`,
  Part Four.
- Potential: sexual partner and romance option.
- Potential: vanishes after a major event, leaving something behind, as an investigation thread.
- She knows more about Caarth than she volunteers, because her sources disagree and she will
  not repeat a set of claims without their distinctions.
- Her wings develop post-strife. A confrontation supplies the last of the priming, and the
  wings come afterward, once she is safe and knows the trouble is over. Quiet evening scene,
  not a mid-fight transformation. See Part Six.

## Relationships to Other NPCs

**Bwavrek:** Her employer. He assigned her the hardest shift and the worst duties and never
acknowledged that she handled them without complaint. She considers this fair, and she reads
his widening access to keys and stores as the acknowledgment it is. She talks about him easily
and warmly right up until the subject touches what she owes him.

**Tavo-Pavo:** He checks on her more than his business warrants. She has noticed and has never
said anything about it. She brings him things from the baths, and she brought him a heat-stone
once when his wrists were bad.

**Nautinto:** Nautinto has never said aloud that she is watching Minttärä. Minttärä has never
said aloud that she knows. Both keep it this way.

**The Library of Caarth:** Not a person, though present in her. She will talk about the Library
happily and at length as an institution, and she goes quiet the moment the subject turns to her
own unfinished business with it.

## Sexual Tone

**At work, she is confident.** The massage is her trade and she is good at it, so her
professional register is warm, playful, and lightly teasing, with the giggle close to the
surface. This is already canon in the intro scene and it should stay.

**With someone she actually likes, she is flustered.** The chatter speeds up, she narrates
what she is doing partly because she talks when she is nervous, and she checks in more than she
needs to. Then it lands, and she goes quiet, and the quiet is the tell that she has stopped
performing.

Her attention is her warmth. She notices what works and adjusts, and she remembers it next
time, which is its own kind of statement from someone who remembers everything.

Afterward the chatter comes back, a little too fast, until she settles.

**Hard limits for writers:** Do not write her as coy or withholding, because she is neither.
Do not write her as fragile, since she is talkative by nature rather than by nerves, and she
has carried three cycles of hard work without complaint. Her uncertainty is about herself and
never about her competence, so she should never sound unsure of her own hands.
