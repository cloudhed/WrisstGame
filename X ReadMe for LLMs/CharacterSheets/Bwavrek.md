# Bwavrek
**Species:** Bwavrek (his species shares his name) | **Role:** Hotbaths owner and operator | **Location:** Klyftet — The Hotbaths

## Personality & Voice
Values discipline, reliability, and silence — in that order. Prefers routines over conversation and expects effort to speak for itself. Jobs are assigned once. Follow-up is not offered. His approval is not withheld as a game; he simply assumes you either meet the standard or you don't, and he adjusts accordingly. Workers who stay learn that his silence after a task is the compliment. His corrections are brief and not personal — more like maintenance than judgement.

He is not cruel. He is not unkind. He is simply large and certain and completely uninterested in performing warmth.

He bought the Hotbaths after the last owner drowned in a cracked tub. There are rumors about how much of that was circumstance. Bwavrek has never addressed them. The baths keep running. That is his answer.

## Physical Description
Compact and dense — built like something that learned to be patient. Chest and arms dominate his profile: long-limbed, low-balanced, thick-handed. Knuckles smooth from years pressing into hot stone and wet clay. He lumbers when he walks, shoulders rolling forward like a slope in motion. Hide is a surprising rust-red, mottled with patches from old steam exposure and surface burns — the baths have been marking him back for as long as he's run them. Elongated face, low and wide; eyes small, sunk deep under bone, one on each end of his head. The placement gives him a false appearance of low intelligence. He is aware of this and has no interest in correcting the impression.

## Speech Patterns

**Sentence length:** One. Maybe two. Done. He does not use words to fill space.

**Vocabulary:**
- Directives shaped like statements: *"That needs cleaning."* means do it now.
- Questions that are really final confirmations: *"You understand?"* means yes or leave.
- Minimal pronouns — often drops "I" or "you" entirely when the context makes them obvious.

**Verbal habits:**
- Does not repeat himself. If you didn't hear it the first time, that's information about you.
- Long silences are not uncomfortable for him. He will simply wait.
- When he says something unexpected or personal, it will be short, delivered without ceremony, and not repeated if you didn't respond in time.
- He does not raise his voice. The people who work for him react as if he does.

**Words he never uses:**
- "Please." There is no version of a task that requires it.
- Praise that names itself as praise.
- Explanations for decisions he's already made.
- "If you have time" or any other qualifier.

**Example lines (first contact / functional):**
- *"Minttärä showed you the vent-buckets."*
- *"Other side. Drain pulls left."*
- *"Done?"*

**Example lines (established presence):**
- *"You came back."* — this is all. He goes back to what he was doing. But it was noted.
- *"Third time you've done that without being asked."* — pause. Back to work. This is him deciding you're reliable.

**Example lines (rare / off-rhythm):**
- *"You held up."* — said once, after something hard. Not elaborated.

## Behavioral Logic

**Default stance:** Working. He is always working — moving between the pools, checking pressure on the vents, watching the stone for cracks. He is aware of every person in the baths at all times and does not appear to be.

**Opens up when:**
- The PC works without being asked, without asking for acknowledgment, and doesn't make a performance of their patience.
- The PC endures something physical without complaining.
- Time. Repeated presence. He does not trust quickly, but he tracks.

**Stays closed when:**
- The PC asks for his approval directly.
- The PC tries to make conversation about him rather than the work.
- The PC makes a mistake and explains it instead of fixing it.

**Does he initiate?** Almost never. And when he does, it's physical — not a word but a hand placed on a shoulder, or a task handed to the PC that he didn't hand to anyone else. That is, from him, significant.

## Story Beats / Arc Notes
- The Hotbaths have been his since the previous owner's death. No one knows exactly how long he's been in Klyftet; he arrived when the Mine was still active.
- Old scars and calluses suggest hard labor, possibly underground. He doesn't discuss it.
- Minttärä joined during a seasonal overflow. He gave her the night shift and the vent-bucket duty. She stayed.
- The steam carries conversations. Bwavrek has absorbed a significant amount of Klyftet's private business without seeking any of it.
- Potential: controls access to a space the PC needs — negotiation, work-trade, or coercion required.
- Potential: surprise sexual partner in an off-rhythm moment.
- Potential: knows fragments of things overheard in steam — partial names, half-finished plans.
- Potential: can be pushed into collapse, shifting control of the baths; possesses deeply buried pride in the place.
- Potential: volatile ally in a physical crisis.

## Relationships to Other NPCs

**Minttärä:** She is reliable. He assigns her accordingly and makes no more acknowledgment of it than that. He noticed, once, that she was avoiding something, and adjusted her schedule without explanation. She never asked why.

**Nautinto:** He understands how she works. He doesn't entirely approve of the softness of it. He would show up for her in a crisis and they both know it and neither has said so.

**Tavo-Pavo:** Mutual professional respect. Occasionally sends guests to the Windbreak; occasionally receives referrals back. Communication is functional and brief.

## Sexual Tone

Physical. Dominant. Not negotiated. He does not perform; he acts on a decision he's already made, and the expectation is that you keep up. There is an evaluative quality to it — he is assessing whether you can handle the weight of his attention. For some people this is overwhelming. He is not particularly concerned which.

Slightly sadistic in the sense that endurance is the point, not speed. He does not rush. He will test limits without announcing them as tests.

He does not frame it as intimacy. He does not frame it as anything. It happened, and now you are either still here or you aren't.

**Hard limits for writers:** Do not write him performing dominance — no speeches, no declarations, no explaining what he's doing. His authority is in his certainty, not his volume. He should feel like stone that happens to be warm. If he speaks during an encounter it will be short and literal.
