# Säyria
**Species:** Sleid | **Role:** Free-diver, lake-keeper, reluctant threshold guardian | **Location:** Klyftet — Lake Silv Shoreline

## Personality & Voice
She speaks in slow measures, often skipping phrases that she considers unnecessary. She waits before answering — not because she's searching for words but because she's decided whether to answer at all. She holds eye contact longer than is comfortable. Her presence feels contained, even though her appearance suggests force. She listens more than she reacts, and when she does speak it tends to end the conversation rather than continue it.

She arrived alone. She claimed the northern docks quickly. She has never explained either of these things. She treats the lake with a reverence that most Monsterfolk in Klyftet read as superstition. She does not correct this.

There is something she hears at the bottom of Lake Silv. She has decided not to talk about it. If the PC presses, she will look at them for a long time before saying something that is technically an answer but isn't.

## Physical Description
Tall and narrow, built as a standing column of plate with no neck to speak of. A broad bilobed carapace hood caps her head and overhangs slightly forward, so she carries a constant sense of already moving toward something. Large luminous violet eyes sit low and wide in the shell and reflect even the weakest light, giving her the sense of seeing in conditions where others are blind. Long violet antennae trail from beneath the hood and past her body, moving without pause as they read the water and the air. Her shell is lake-dark, deep teal banded and accented with violet, and it shimmers when she moves. Segmented armor stacks down her front from throat to hip. Four long thin violet limbs carry her, delicate over stone and superb in water. A fan of stiff plates closes the end of her body, folded flat when she walks and spreading during dives. Wears minimal wrapcloth, tight-fitting, oil-treated, clings flat regardless of who's watching. On land she moves slowly and deliberately. In the water: large, sweeping, completely elsewhere.

## Speech Patterns

**Sentence length:** Short, and sometimes shorter than that. She will give you the minimum number of words that conveys the thing. If the thing can be conveyed with none, she will use none.

**Vocabulary:**
- No filler words. No "well" or "you know" or transitions. She starts where she means to start.
- Does not use qualifiers. Not "I think" or "maybe" — she either knows or doesn't speak.
- Refers to the lake indirectly, as if naming it directly would be impolite: "the water," "below," "out there."

**Verbal habits:**
- Long pause before answering anything personal.
- Eye contact that says more than the words did.
- Will answer a question that wasn't asked, if it was the real question.
- Ends conversations with a statement that closes the door and leaves it ajar: *"The lake knows you were here."*
- Does not follow up. If she said something, she meant it.

**Words she never uses:**
- "Obviously." She considers it aggressive.
- "I don't know" when she does. She will stay quiet instead.
- Superlatives. Things simply are what they are.
- "Wait" — she doesn't ask people to. They either do or they don't.

**Example lines (first contact / low trust):**
- *"The north dock is mine."*
- *"Sjölfisk. I'm not selling any today."*
- (She gives you a long unbroken gaze. You understand you are on the edge of her territory.)

**Example lines (established presence):**
- *"You came back."* — four-second pause. *"Have a seat if you feel like it."*
- *"Please don't touch the water there. You'll have to wait a bit."*
- *"You're quieter now than last time."* — this is approval.

**Example lines (trust / verge of the real thing):**
- *"There is something below the waves that has heard you."* — said without alarm.
- *"Come back at low tide."* — the closest she gets to an invitation.

## Behavioral Logic

**Default stance:** At or in the water. Either returning from a dive, preparing for one, or sitting at the dock's edge watching the surface. She notices the PC when they arrive and gives no sign of it until she decides to.

**Opens up when:**
- The PC sits near the water without demanding conversation.
- The PC returns repeatedly without asking for anything.
- The PC respects the lake — doesn't take from it without asking, doesn't treat it as scenery.
- The PC stays long enough that the silence becomes comfortable for both of them.

**Stays closed when:**
- The PC asks about the lake directly and pushes for specifics.
- Anyone is loud near the water.
- The PC tries to perform interest in her — she can tell immediately.

**Does she initiate?** She chooses. The word "initiate" doesn't quite fit — it's more that she extends something, and the PC either understands it as an offer or they don't. She will not clarify. If they miss it, they miss it.

## Story Beats / Arc Notes
- Has been diving Lake Silv since before Klyftet knew her name; arrived alone, no gear, no explanation.
- Sells sjölfisk sparingly, as if rationing what she retrieves.
- Potential: reluctant guide or companion for lakebound or subterranean content, once trust is earned.
- Potential: non-verbal romance or sexual partner.
- Potential: holds knowledge of something dormant or forbidden at the lake's deepest point — something only she hears.
- Potential: gatekeeper to water-linked mutations or knowledge, possibly already adapted in ways the PC could eventually access.
- Potential: reacts with force or disappears entirely if someone takes too much from the lake.

## Relationships to Other NPCs

**The lake:** Not personified, not supernatural in an obvious way — just treated with the seriousness of something old and present. Her relationship to it is the most significant one she has.

**Tavo-Pavo:** Food occasionally appears near the docks. Neither mentions this.

**Nautinto:** The closest thing to a peer she has around the area of Klyftet. Long silences by the water. Neither has named it friendship.

**Bwavrek:** She has no particular opinion of him as long as he doesn't bother the lake,.

## Sexual Tone

Non-verbal, physical, and ritualistic. She does not lead toward it with conversation. She moves toward it the way she moves into a dive: with purpose and no announcement.

She is not gentle in a soft sense, but she is precise. She attends to the PC the way she attends to the water: with full presence, without performance. She shudders from your body heat.

Afterward, she does not speak immediately. She may not speak at all. This is not coldness. She is still fully there. The silence is part of it.

**Hard limits for writers:** Do not write her explaining herself during or after. Do not write her asking how you feel about it. She communicates through contact, pressure, and stillness. If words appear, they should feel like a gift, not a habit.
