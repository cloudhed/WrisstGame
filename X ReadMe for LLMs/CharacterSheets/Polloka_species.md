# Pöllöka
**Type:** Monsterfolk | **Common habitats:** Urban fringe zones, carved-out warrens beneath hill-towns, silk-hut communes near thermal vents

## Overview
A social species known for oversized eyes and distinctive curved protrusions (keratinous, branching fronds) arcing from the back of their skull. Culturally matriarchal but gender-fluid in role and expression. Young Pöllöka do not yet have wings — wings only grow on very old individuals.

## Physical Description
Oversized eyes. Curved keratinous protrusions arc from the back of the skull like hardened fronds — branching from a central crest, flare or fold depending on mood. Upper limbs set high, fold forward along the chest — structured more like folded forelegs than arms. Each ends in a pair of hooked mantis-like claws: precise, used for both craft and communication (very expressive — flicking and tapping while speaking). Antennae near the brows flick and wave constantly, signaling emotional state whether they want to or not. Mandibles capable of clicking sounds; each pattern signals an emotional state. Skin thick and smooth, almost scale-like to touch but without visible pattern. Most wear minimal clothing (thin bands, veils, silk wraps) to keep torso and limbs exposed to air. Voices melodic, full of clicks, low whistles, and rolling consonants.

**Gender distinction:** Females have more flamboyant horn coloration; males have larger horns and deeper vocal timbre.

**Genitalia:** Housed within soft vent folds located low between the thighs. Females: on females close to the tail root — recessed fleshy slit that opens under stimulation, revealing a ridged slick inner canal. Males: closer to the taint — a segmented bulbous penis that unfurls outward in slow pulses when aroused, or inverts inward to form a muscular pocketed canal capable of being penetrated. Both forms self-lubricate with a faintly honeyed secretion.

## Behavior
- Live in non-familial social pods.
- Known to chase their dreams across caste boundaries; ambitious individuals seek trade or guild training away from home.
- Culturally matriarchal but gender-fluid in role and expression.

## Notes on Young Pöllöka
Wings only appear in very old Pöllöka. A young Pöllöka like Minttärä does not have wings yet. This is notable and potentially story-significant (see Minttärä's sheet for a potential arc beat involving early wing growth).
