# Minttärä — Backstory, History, and the Open Route

> **Status: proposal.** Nothing here is canon yet. It is a deep well to pull from when writing
> her scenes, so that a follow-up question from the PC has an answer waiting instead of being
> invented mid-scene. It can be promoted, edited, or dropped wholesale.
>
> Canon sources this must never contradict: `Minttara.md`, `Polloka_species.md`,
> `Bwavrek.md`, `TavoPavo.md`, `Klyftet_location.md`, and the quest structure in `ROADMAP.md`.
> Where this file resolves something canon leaves open, **Part Eight** says so.

---

## How to use this file

**Species facts live in `Polloka_species.md`.** Anatomy, senses, temperature, wings, pods,
pilgrimage, and etiquette were all put there, since they apply to every Pöllöka ever written.
Read that sheet first. This file covers how Minttärä specifically carries, resents, and works
around all of it, and it holds the dialogue.

**Her voice lives in `Minttara.md`.** That sheet is canon and wins on any conflict. This file
adds the history behind the voice, not a replacement for it.

Most sections carry three layers:

> **True:** what is actually the case. Author-level. The PC may never learn it, and it exists so
> that two scenes written eight months apart agree with each other.
>
> **She'd say:** what she would actually put in her own mouth if asked. Close to dialogue-ready.
> Register already correct.
>
> **She doesn't know:** the honest gap. **Use this layer often.** She is a record keeper, so the
> things she cannot verify bother her visibly, and that is more interesting than an answer.

When the PC asks something neither file covers, prefer "she doesn't know" over inventing, then
write the gap down in Part Seven afterward.

# PART ONE — MINTTÄRÄ AT A GLANCE

A warm, chatty record keeper working a bathhouse, three cycles into a journey that was supposed
to take one season, holding an unfinished obligation she has never once asked the size of.

She could walk out tomorrow and nobody would stop her. What holds her is that leaving would
mean leaving an account unsettled, and an unsettled account is the one thing her entire
upbringing taught her not to tolerate. She has been not-deciding for three cycles, which is her
sheet's phrasing and exactly right.

She is good company and she knows it, which makes her easy to spend time with and means the PC
can be halfway into her life before either of them notices. The things she will not discuss
are correspondingly obvious, since a person who talks this readily going quiet is impossible to
miss.

## Appearance

Warm ochre integument across most of the body, with deep teal on the foreclaws, the feet, the
cranial fronds, and the ridges behind her shoulders. Bright cyan eyes beneath clear protective
membranes. Female, and therefore on the larger side for a Pöllöka, with a lower and fuller
voice than the males around her, which suits a person who spends words carefully.

Her fronds curl inward near the tips, and one side carries a shallow worn groove where she has
rested a record cord against it while working for most of her adult life. Her thighs are
unusually broad even for her species, built up over three cycles of Klyftet's slopes and the
vent-buckets.

She wears thin green wraps fastened at both hips. The left fastening holds her route-silk,
folded so the blank closing panel sits against her body. The right holds a short work strip
marked with maintenance knots.

Recently her upper-back clothing has begun to sit looser, which she has not explained to
anyone.

# PART TWO — BEFORE WRISST

## The Low Amber Broodhouse

> **True:** She emerged at the Low Amber Broodhouse, a communal nursery built around a mineral
> steam vent and shared by several neighbouring pods. Her cohort held seven larvae. She handled
> physical play badly and kept to the edges of the chamber, so caretakers started handing her
> feeding counts and temperature markers to arrange, mostly to give her something to do. She
> turned out to remember which caretaker had placed each item, even after the objects were
> moved. That is the origin of everything she later became.

Her biological mother, **Elvärä**, belongs to the Sixth Vent Pod and works with textile dyes.
She visited throughout Minttärä's larval growth and gave her coloured eye-veils after her first
eclosion. They remain fond of each other, and Elvärä never expected her to join the Sixth.

Her paternal record carries a valid mark from a travelling route surveyor who left the
settlement before she emerged. Two route letters from him sit in the Low Amber records. She has
never met him as an adult and has never made an issue of it.

> **She'd say:** "Elvärä dyes cloth, which is why I'm insufferable about green. I can tell you
> four of them apart and nobody has ever once wanted me to."
>
> **She doesn't know:** whether her father is alive, and she has not tried to find out. She will
> say the record is open and that she has no plans for it, cheerfully enough, and then change
> the subject faster than she changes most subjects.

## The Ninth Vent Pod

After her first eclosion she spent guest rotations with four pods and chose the Ninth, because
its record room stayed active through the night and its members let her work without talking at
her.

**Sävellä** holds the Ninth's vent key. She speaks slowly during disputes and requires every
uncertain claim to stay visibly marked in the final record, even when the marking is
inconvenient. Minttärä took that habit whole and still has it.

**Kerrikki** is an older male record keeper and her craft teacher. His eye plates had begun
clouding by the time she came to him, so he checked copied records by raised stitching,
punctures, resin scent, and the spacing of knots. From him she learned to prepare a record for
several kinds of inspection at once, which is why she carves tactile labels into shelves in a
bathhouse where nobody has asked her to.

> **True:** Her first real piece of work was a missing container of broodhouse grain fat. Three
> caretakers each remembered moving it and each named a different storage room. She compared
> entry scents, work rotations, and the pressure marks left under the container, and found it
> behind a replacement wall panel. What got her noticed was not the find. It was that she
> preserved all three wrong accounts with their original source markings instead of discarding
> them once she had the answer.
>
> **She'd say:** "Three people remembered it differently, and all three of them were telling the
> truth about what they remembered, which is the bit everybody skips. So I kept all three. It
> was behind a wall panel, by the way. Nobody had moved it anywhere."

## The pilgrimage she declared

Kerrikki used the grain fat case when he recommended her. Her declared purpose was to reach the
**Library of Caarth** and develop a practical method for comparing conflicting route histories
recorded by different Monsterfolk, then bring the method home.

The problem is real and she can explain it in one breath: Pöllöka records carry their source in
the record itself, and almost nobody else's do. When a Nöteri, a Kraaal, and a Pöllöka each
describe the same road, only one of those accounts says how its author knows.

Her promised service at the Library was copying water-damaged travel records and building a
tactile index for collections held only in visual script. Sävellä opened the route. Kerrikki
added the craft sponsor mark.

> **She doesn't know:** whether the method she proposed is possible. She has never seen the
> Library's holdings and suspects the proposal was more ambitious than it was informed.

# PART THREE — THE CROSSING, AND THE ARRIVAL

> **True:** She reached the coast with her route-silk, a travel harness, two heat stones, food,
> and a small contribution from the Ninth. She cut the cost of passage by working the crossing
> as a manifest clerk, which is the detail that later matters most, because it means she read
> the cargo lists.

Rough water pushed the vessel off its intended landing. Part of the hold flooded, which ruined
one heat stone and soaked the outer section of her route-silk, stiffening it with salt so the
witness marks along that stretch can no longer be read. Her food pouch went missing during the
unloading and she does not know whether it was taken or lost.

The crew put her ashore at a western trade landing in Wrisst with no continuing passage. A
freight carrier bound for Klyftet told her the mountain settlement connected with northern
caravans running toward Caarth, which was true when he said it.

She could not afford a heated berth, and the exposed cargo rack would have put her into torpor
before the pass. The carrier had a standing supply account with **Bwavrek**, who approved an
advance covering her passage, a replacement heat stone, food, and her first nights of lodging.
She marked the agreement before getting into the wagon.

She arrived cold, hungry, and unable to keep her antennae extended in the split-howl. Bwavrek
put her beside a heated wall and left food within claw reach, and waited until the following
night before showing her anything in a ledger.

> **She'd say:** "The water took the middle of it. Everything the sea touched went stiff, so the
> marks are still there and you can't read any of them. I can feel roughly where they were,
> which is not the same thing at all."
>
> **She doesn't know:** what happened to her food pouch. She will not guess out loud, and she is
> apologetic rather than stern about it, since a guess said in front of someone has a way of
> becoming a claim later.

The northern route then proved unreliable. Mine traffic fell off, caravans left irregularly,
and several carriers refused solitary passengers. By the time a workable route appeared, she
still owed part of the advance. Her packed travel harness stayed under her sleeping mat, and it
is still there.

# PART FOUR — THE HOTBATHS, AND THE DEBT NOBODY HAS NAMED

## The knot

> **True, and this is the centre of SQ01:** Bwavrek keeps an honest ledger. The advance is
> recorded accurately, no interest has ever been added, and her food and sleeping space are
> entered separately before each work credit is applied. Anyone could inspect it. It would take
> Minttärä, who is a trained record keeper, roughly a minute to read.
>
> **She has never asked what the total is.** Not once in three cycles. **He has never said it
> aloud either.**

Both halves of that need a reason, and both have one.

**Her side.** By training, not asking is absurd, and she knows it. A recorded debt is a fact
you inspect. But she did not arrive as a record keeper, she arrived unable to hold her antennae
up, and he gave her a warm wall and food before he gave her a number. Asking for the total now
would convert that into a transaction, and she will not be the one who does that. So the
trained instinct loses to the arrival, every time she thinks about it, for three cycles.

**His side.** Naming a figure to a worker who has not asked for one is pressure, and Bwavrek
does not apply pressure to people who are performing. He acknowledges good work by widening
access instead, handing over keys, stores, and maintenance equipment, and expects that to be
understood. Minttärä does understand it. Neither of them will say so.

The result is an accurate ledger, an honest employer, a worker who could read it in a minute,
and no number spoken aloud in three cycles.

> **The PC's actual job in SQ01 is not an audit.** It is getting a number said out loud between
> two people who both consider asking to be beneath them. The fraud angle does not exist,
> because there is no fraud.

> **She'd say**, asked what she owes, easily and with no weight on it at all:
> "The passage, and the stone, and the food from when I got here. He didn't have to do any of
> that, so."
>
> Asked how much it comes to, and this is the hinge of the whole quest:
> "Oh..! I've never asked what my debt comes to..."
>
> Asked why not, immediately afterward:
> nothing. She has no answer, because the question is three seconds old to her. She finds
> something that needs doing.
>
> Later, once it has had time to work on her:
> "I still don't have a good answer for why I never asked. I've been turning it over."

**Writing note.** She is surprised, and she is not dodging. The blind spot sits exactly where her
expertise is, which is why it survived three cycles: she knows precisely how to audit an
account, and auditing this one would turn the night Bwavrek took her in into a transaction, so
she stopped being able to see that she was avoiding it.

**The PC's ordinary question is what breaks the stalemate.** Not investigation, not leverage.
Everyone else in Klyftet already knows the situation and stopped seeing it years ago, and it
takes someone with no context to ask the obvious thing out loud. Keep the PC's line plain, and
let the weight come entirely from her reaction to it.

**The reason is unexplored rather than guarded**, so it cannot be extracted in the same
conversation. It forms offscreen between visits, which gives the quest natural progression
without a relationship meter: come back later and she is further along.

> **Bwavrek would say**, asked the same thing:
> "She has not asked me. I do not put numbers in front of people who have not asked for them."

## The vent-bucket accident

The Hotbaths regulates water into one of the steam channels with a suspended bucket, held by a
metal catch that stops the loaded rope unspooling when the worker changes grip.

Minttärä noticed the catch shifting under weight and marked the fault twice on the maintenance
strip, then told Bwavrek the second groove was no longer holding. He tested it by hand with the
bucket empty, and it caught, so he put off replacement until the next supply delivery.

Several nights later the pressure under her dorsal plates interrupted her claw control while
she was raising a full bucket. Her left hook missed its grip, the worn catch failed to engage,
and the bucket dropped into the channel housing. The impact cracked the housing and closed part
of the Hotbaths for two nights.

The repair cost something real. Bwavrek added it to her account, because in his reading the
operator carries a dropped load. She accepts that her missed grip contributed. She disputes
carrying the whole of it, and she has said so exactly once.

A pale abrasion crosses the inner hook of her left foreclaw where the rope slipped. The broken
catch is still in a storeroom behind the linen room, and its inner surface has a smooth wear
channel that can be felt with a claw or read by anyone who knows metal.

> **She'd say:** "I missed the rope, and that part's mine. The catch had already slipped twice
> though, and I marked it both times, so an account that's any use has to carry both of those."
>
> **She doesn't know:** what the repair cost. She has not asked that either, and by now she
> finds that genuinely difficult to explain to herself, which is the closest she comes to being
> annoyed with her own habits.

## Klyftet, and the people in it

**Bwavrek.** Employer, and the closest thing she has to a matriarch's function without any of
a matriarch's obligations. He controls the work, the rooms, and the stores, and carries none of
the formal duties that would come with that at home. She has noticed this and has never held it
against him, because he has never pretended otherwise. She has never marked a threshold plate
here, so her sleeping alcove is familiar and physically suitable and carries no promise of
continued warmth.

**Tavo-Pavo.** He fed her the night she arrived, after the Hotbaths kitchen had closed. She now
collects grain scraps and damaged utensils from the Windbreak, returns the utensils repaired,
and leaves a punctured count of everything she takes. He checks on her more than his business
warrants and she has never said anything about it. He knows about the debt through ordinary
Klyftet gossip and has offered to confront Bwavrek, which she refused.

**Nautinto.** Watching her, silently, by mutual unacknowledged agreement. Nautinto is the one
person in Klyftet who could tell her what her upper back is doing and why, and has not raised it.

**Rupaaa & Baaaku.** Barely acquainted. Their tactile sensing makes them the obvious people to
read the wear channel on the broken catch, and they would charge for it, or take a favour, or
waive it if the PC has already shown interest in the Old Mine. That is a light optional link
into SQ03 that leaves both quests independent.

## Her routine now

She wakes shortly before sunset and eats beside the hottest wall, pressing her upper back
against the stone until the pressure eases. She washes her foot pads before crossing a clean
floor. Her first round covers the water channels, the vent shutters, the linen store, and the
bucket mechanism. Through the busy hours she stays near the service passages and enters public
rooms only when a customer needs something or the steam pressure changes.

She eats several small portions through the night, and Tavo's kitchen scraps go faster than
they used to. After closing she copies the salt-damaged sections of her route-silk onto ordinary
cloth, which cannot take a formal seal, so the substitute is a practice run and the real closing
panel stays blank.

She sleeps in a narrow alcove behind the linen room, with her travel harness, her eye veil, the
ruined heat stone, and her Library correspondence packed underneath the mat.

# PART FIVE — CAARTH

> ⚠️ **This part makes main-quest decisions and is the least safe material in the file.** It
> hands the PC a destination and a way in. Read Part Eight before using any of it.

> **True:** She has never reached Caarth and has no direct observation from inside the city.
> Everything she has is second-hand, and she knows exactly which parts are which, which is the
> whole reason she has not repeated any of it.

What she is holding:

- Her acceptance from the Library named a **lower intake** for visiting record workers, rather
  than the public receiving hall. Witnessed, in her own possession, sewn into the protected
  inner fold of her route-silk.
- A second Library notice arrived shortly before she departed, saying the **public receiving
  hall had closed**, with no reason given. Its own explanation carried the Pöllöka mark for
  reconstruction from several sources, so even the Library was passing on something it had not
  witnessed.
- The freight crew told her **Caarth's harbour traffic had stopped**. Reported speech, from
  people with a reason to explain a diverted landing.
- **A manifest she personally handled** recorded sealed cargo accepted at Caarth four nights
  after the traffic supposedly stopped. Directly witnessed, by her, in writing she processed
  herself.

The last two do not agree, and the one she witnessed is the one that contradicts the story she
was told. Something was still reaching Caarth under an arrangement that was not public.

> **She doesn't know:** who controls those arrangements, why public access changed, whether the
> lower intake is still open, or whether the Library is even still receiving. Her most recent
> information is three cycles old and she says so before she says anything else.
>
> **She'd say**, once she trusts the PC as a witness: "Two of those came from people who wanted
> me to believe them. One came off a manifest I wrote out myself. I'd start with the manifest,
> though I would say that, wouldn't I."

She keeps the contradiction to herself at first because every source in it carries a different
reliability, and repeating the set without the distinctions would be, to her, a lie. Showing
the PC the two Library notices gives them a destination and a probable entrance without
explaining anything about what happened around the city.

# PART SIX — HER WINGS

Species mechanics are in `Polloka_species.md`. This is her specific state.

> **True:** Her matrices are heavily primed and have been for some time. The failed crossing,
> the involuntary rerouting, the cold arrival, three cycles of night labour, and an unresolved
> obligation have supplied more than enough sustained strain. The Hotbaths supplies the warmth
> and humidity. What is missing is the second condition, since the strain has never actually
> ended, and her body will not begin while she is still carrying it.

Symptoms already visible to a PC who is paying attention, in rough order of subtlety:

- She presses her upper back against heated stone whenever she stops moving.
- Her upper-back clothing sits looser than it did.
- She eats through the night and does not explain the appetite.
- Her frond bases stay dark after physical work, longer than the work accounts for.
- She has become protective of the private steam chamber.
- She will not let anyone stand close behind her.
- One foreclaw occasionally misses its grip, which is what caused the accident.

**The trigger is the settlement, not the fight.** Whatever violence or confrontation the arc
puts her through supplies the last of the priming, and the wings stay under the plates while it
is happening. They begin once she is safe and has accepted that the thing is over. Clearing the
account can do it. So can choosing to stay at the Hotbaths under terms she negotiated, since
her body is responding to recovered control rather than to departure.

Her wings come in with deep teal veins and pale green membranes, with warm ochre scales
gathering toward the outer edges. The lower pair repeats the pattern tighter.

The first unmistakable sign is a thin line of moisture along her upper dorsal seam, with the
plate edges standing a few millimetres off her back while she leans against the steam chamber
wall. That is an evening scene, quiet, after everything is settled.

# PART SEVEN — VOICE, AND EVERYTHING SHE DOES NOT KNOW

## Writing her

`Minttara.md` is canon and governs, and it holds the full voice, the two registers, and the
example lines. Four things this file adds on top of it.

**Her sourcing habit is endearing rather than clinical.** She splits a sentence another
character would leave whole, keeping what she witnessed apart from what she was told, and in
her warm register it plays as someone who would feel awful about accidentally misleading you.
It never appears as clicks or notation in the text, only as the habit. See the Speech and
sourcing section of `Polloka_species.md`.

**She will not overstate a claim even when it helps her.** If the PC exaggerates on her behalf,
she corrects it, out loud, in front of whoever is listening, at cost to herself, and she is
apologetic while doing it. This is the single most useful lever in her characterization and
should get at least one scene.

**Praise turns into measurement.** Compliment the clean pools and she tells you how many filters
she replaced, cheerfully and at length, as though the number settles it. The sourcing habit
explains why: a compliment is an unsupported claim, and she answers claims with evidence.

**Her competence is never in doubt, only her direction.** She is fluent and confident about
water, heat, records, and her own hands, and she hedges about what she wants. Never write her
sounding unsure of her work.

When she is angry, everything reverses. The antennae stop roaming and draw close against her
fronds, the chatter cuts out entirely, and she drops every optional word, which from someone
who normally uses all of them is genuinely alarming. She puts herself physically between the
other person and whatever is being disputed.

## Everything she does not know

Use this list. It is more useful than the answers.

- What she owes, in numbers.
- What the housing repair cost.
- Whether the Library still receives visitors, or still stands as she was told it did.
- Who was moving sealed cargo into Caarth, or why.
- What happened to her food pouch.
- Whether her father is alive.
- What is happening to her upper back. She has a suspicion and no confirmation, and she has not
  asked Nautinto, who would know.
- Whether she still wants Caarth, or whether she wants the version of herself that was going
  there.
- Whether Bwavrek would have let her leave at any point in the last three cycles, since she has
  never tested it.

# PART EIGHT — OPEN DECISIONS

Everything here is unruled. Nothing in the rest of this file should be treated as settled until
these are.

**1. The Caarth material (Part Five).** The biggest one. The lower intake, the closed receiving
hall, and the freight manifest contradiction are main-quest facts invented inside a
side-character document. Adopting them means the PC's route into Caarth comes from Minttärä,
which is a strong story shape and also a real MQ commitment. `ROADMAP.md` is the authority and
does not currently say any of this. **Rule on this before writing a line of it.**

**2. The named home cast.** Elvärä, Sävellä, Kerrikki, the Low Amber Broodhouse, and the Ninth
Vent Pod. All invented here. They become canon the moment she says one aloud, and Kerrikki in
particular is load-bearing, since he is the reason she keeps records the way she does.

**3. The parcel from home.** The superseded draft had a courier deliver recording silk and pod
resin so she could repair the salt-damaged silk, with Tavo sending the PC as the carrier. It is
a clean quest opening and it is also an invented plot device. Keep, replace, or drop.

**4. The vent-bucket accident.** Good, because her wing symptoms cause the accident, which ties
her body to her quest. It also fixes an event in the recent past that other scenes will have to
respect. Confirm or replace.

**5. Whether Bwavrek is right.** As written he is honest, he did test the catch, he tested it
badly, and he genuinely believes the operator carries a dropped load. That makes SQ01 a story
about two people being reasonable rather than a story with a villain. Confirm that is the tone
wanted.

**6. Nautinto's silence.** Part Four has her recognizing Minttärä's dorsal symptoms and saying
nothing. That is a strong beat and it makes Nautinto complicit in a way that needs deliberate
approval.

**7. The manifest clerk detail.** Her working the crossing as a clerk is what gives her the
Caarth manifest. If the Caarth material in Part Five is dropped, this detail loses its purpose
and should go with it.

**8. Superseded material.** The original ChatGPT pass contained roughly four thousand words of
pod governance, succession procedure, settlement councils, death rites, ageing, and music, plus
a full SQ01 quest design with fixed debt figures and an evidence-gathering structure. All of it
was cut. The debt figures specifically are void under the ruling in Part Four. The original is
archived at `Archive/superseded_docs/` if any of it is wanted back.
